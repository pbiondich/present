Working together · What it buys you

Agree on the shared rails,
and serve your people your own way

If you lead a province

  • Patients arrive with their history, and leave with it too
  • Your results reflect the care you gave, with each patient counted once
  • Your records outlast any one system or supplier
  • You choose what suits your facilities, and help govern what is shared

If you build applications

  • Show your users what happened elsewhere, not only what you recorded
  • Use the shared lists of people, places and codes, and stop maintaining your own
  • Build one connection, and it works in every province
  • Put your effort into the care your users deliver, not the plumbing

One patient, one record, one connected system
